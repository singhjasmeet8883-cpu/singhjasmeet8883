Digital Marketing

Understanding the Core Strategies of Online Marketing

Digital Marketing is the use of digital channels, platforms, and technologies to promote products, services, and brands to consumers. Below are the major pillars of digital marketing.

🔍

Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

Improving website visibility on search engines like Google.

  • On-page SEO
  • Off-page SEO
  • Technical SEO
  • Keyword Research
  • Link Building
📱

Social Media Marketing

Promoting brands through social media platforms.

  • Facebook & Instagram Ads
  • Content Creation
  • Community Engagement
  • Influencer Marketing
  • Analytics & Insights
✍️

Content Marketing

Creating valuable content to attract and engage audience.

  • Blogs & Articles
  • Videos & Reels
  • Infographics
  • E-books & Guides
  • Storytelling
💰

Pay-Per-Click (PPC)

Paid advertising where you pay only when someone clicks.

  • Google Ads
  • Facebook Ads
  • Display Advertising
  • Remarketing
  • Budget Management
📧

Email Marketing

Direct communication with customers through emails.

  • Newsletter Campaigns
  • Automated Sequences
  • Personalization
  • A/B Testing
  • List Building
📊

Web Analytics

Measuring and analyzing website & campaign performance.

  • Google Analytics
  • Conversion Tracking
  • User Behavior
  • ROI Measurement
  • Heatmaps & Reports
